A bit about how I work

I've spent the last seven years helping small brands figure out what they look like — starting with a logo, but usually ending up somewhere between a website, a packaging system, and a set of rules nobody breaks. I studied graphic design in Kathmandu and picked up front-end code out of necessity, then stayed because I liked it.

I care most about work that still feels sharp a few years later, not just at launch.
